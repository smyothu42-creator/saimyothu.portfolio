## Cover letter (longer version)

Dear Koru UX Design team,

I'm writing to apply for the UI/UX Designer role. I'm a product designer based in Yangon, Myanmar: co-founder and product designer at OrderFlow, where cafés and tea houses run their table ordering, and a product designer at Heizen, where I designed ordering products for Superfine Kitchen in the Bay Area.

I've been designing for close to four years, almost three of them in product, mostly B2B tools that people use while they're busy and can't afford a wrong step — which is close to the work in your description.

At OrderFlow I designed both sides of the product: the guest ordering app and the café admin that runs every order, including the dashboards owners open at closing — daily and monthly totals, orders by channel, sales per item. One pilot café ran 1,300+ orders in its first 42 days, for 35.7M MMK. After watching a real shift, I rebuilt the order hand-off so a counter order reaches the kitchen in about 1–10 seconds with one click; in testing, completing an order without errors went from 60% to 100%. I also work directly in the codebase when a detail needs to be built, not only specified.

At Heizen, I researched and designed Superfine Kitchen's company-lunch ordering flow — interviews, journey maps, and a responsive flow that shows the allowance, personal cost and cut-off before checkout. In its first six weeks it handled 1,738 orders worth $173K across 49 companies. I also ran a WCAG 2.2 AA audit over 17 desktop and phone screens, fixed the 16 issues I found, and the 36 automated checks came back clean.

Design systems are where I've spent most of the last year. For MTB Pay's merchant app (100+ merchants, live on both app stores) I built the Figma system on Carbon, with tokens, variants and usage notes. Now I'm leading Flowy at OrderFlow: shared tokens, written component contracts, a React package and a 21-component Storybook catalog for two live products, with rules that designers, developers and coding agents can all follow.

What draws me to Koru is the healthcare focus. The people I design for are often on their feet, reading in a second language, or new to digital tools: older guests looking for search, shopkeepers learning a wallet, staff during a rush. In a clinic or an enterprise health system those constraints get sharper — plain language, clear states, no dead ends, errors that are easy to undo — and that's the kind of problem I want to keep working on with a team that treats it as a specialty.

I work in Figma, prototype in code when it helps, and I write up my decisions with the evidence behind them. I'm based in Yangon (GMT+6:30), one hour ahead of IST, and available to work remotely.

My portfolio is at [portfolio URL]; the OrderFlow, Superfine Kitchen and Flowy cases are the closest to this role.

Thank you for your time.
